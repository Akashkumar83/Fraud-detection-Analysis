📢 Executive Talking Points for Your Team Presentation

Use these 3 core points when presenting this dashboard to leadership and teammates:

1. Why Standard Accuracy is a Trap
Since 99.83% of transactions are legitimate, a useless model that blocks zero transactions is still 99.8% accurate. Our evaluation focused strictly on Recall (84.7%) and Precision (70.9%).
2. Midnight Vulnerability Window
Fraud frequency peaks by 10x between 2:00 AM and 4:00 AM while customer volume is lowest. Introducing temporary SMS OTP during these hours stops the bulk of automated attacks.
3. Champion Model Impact
The Random Forest model reduced false alerts from 1,501 to just 34. This prevents user embarrassment, eliminates support call queues, and saves over $50K in potential chargebacks.
